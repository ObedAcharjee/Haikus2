Haikus

Haiku is a type of short-form poetry that originated in Japan.
Traditional Japanese haiku consists of three phrases composed of 17 syllables in a 5, 7, 5 pattern.

There is more to Haikus than just adhering to the syllable count; however, I have decided to take a different approach and base mine on feelings, emotions, or observations.
